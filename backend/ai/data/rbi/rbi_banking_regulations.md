# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Directions & Regulatory Guidelines for Banking

## 1. Electronic Payment Systems & Transfer Limits
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface)**:
  - Standard transaction limit set by NPCI / RBI is up to ₹1,00,000 (one lakh rupees) per 24-hour cycle for standard peer-to-peer (P2P) and peer-to-merchant (P2M) transfers.
  - Enhanced UPI limit of ₹5,00,000 is permitted for verified educational institutions, healthcare hospitals, IPO subscriptions, and Retail Direct Treasury Bills.
  - UPI Lite enables pinless small-value transactions up to ₹500 per transaction with an on-device wallet limit of ₹2,000.
- **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)**:
  - IMPS functions 24x7x365 in real-time.
  - Per-transaction limit is ₹5,00,000 (five lakh rupees). Banks may establish internal sub-limits based on customer risk profiling.
- **NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)**:
  - Available round the clock (24x7x365) in half-hourly settlement batches.
  - There is no minimum or maximum limit imposed by the RBI for NEFT fund transfers.
- **RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)**:
  - Operates 24x7x365 for large-value continuous gross settlements.
  - The minimum transaction limit for RTGS is ₹2,00,000 (two lakh rupees). There is no upper ceiling on RTGS transfers.

## 2. Customer Liability in Unauthorized Electronic Banking Transactions (RBI Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18)
- **Zero Liability of a Customer**:
  - A customer has zero liability when unauthorized transactions occur due to contributory fraud, negligence, or deficiency on the part of the bank (irrespective of whether the customer reports it or not).
  - A customer has zero liability in third-party breaches where the deficiency lies neither with the bank nor with the customer, provided the customer notifies the bank within **3 working days** of receiving the transaction alert.
- **Limited Liability of a Customer**:
  - If a customer reports the unauthorized transaction between **4 to 7 working days** after receiving the alert, the customer's maximum liability is capped as follows:
    - Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) / Jan Dhan accounts: Maximum ₹1,000.
    - Other savings accounts, prepaid instruments, and credit cards with limits up to ₹5 lakh: Maximum ₹5,000.
    - All other accounts (current accounts, cash credit, credit cards above ₹5 lakh limit): Maximum ₹10,000.
  - If the delay in reporting exceeds **7 working days**, customer liability is determined in accordance with the individual bank's Board-approved policy.
- **Fraud Reporting Helplines**:
  - Customers must immediately register cyber financial fraud on National Cybercrime Helpline **1930** and at **cybercrime.gov.in**.

## 3. Debit Card & Credit Card Management Guidelines
- **Channel Controls & Safety Mandates**:
  - Under RBI Master Direction on Card Issuance, banks must provide cardholders with granular controls via mobile banking and web portals to:
    - Instantly switch ON or OFF domestic transactions (POS / swipe).
    - Toggle international cross-border transactions (Forex).
    - Toggle online e-commerce transactions and e-mandates.
    - Toggle contactless NFC tap-and-pay transactions and ATM cash withdrawals.
  - Enable customers to dynamically modify daily and per-transaction spending limits within sanctioned board limits.
- **Card Tokenization (CoFT - Card-on-File Tokenization)**:
  - Online merchants are strictly prohibited from storing actual 16-digit card numbers, CVVs, or expiry dates. All digital cards must be replaced with encrypted tokens issued by card networks (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay).

## 4. Customer Due Diligence (KYC) Norms
- **Official Valid Documents (OVD)**:
  - Valid OVDs for identity and address verification include: Passport, Driving Licence, Proof of possession of Aadhaar number, Voter's Identity Card, Job card issued by NREGA, and Letter issued by National Population Register.
- **Periodic KYC Updation**:
  - High-risk customers: KYC update required at least once every **2 years**.
  - Medium-risk customers: KYC update required once every **8 years**.
  - Low-risk customers: KYC update required once every **10 years**.
  - Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is recognized as a valid digital method for full KYC verification without branch visits.

## 5. Deposit Accounts, Interest Rates & Insurance Cover
- **Deposit Insurance (DICGC)**:
  - Under Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) regulations, deposits across savings, current, fixed deposits (FD), and recurring deposits (RD) are insured up to a maximum of **₹5,00,000** (five lakh rupees) per depositor across all branches of a single bank.
- **Senior Citizen Benefits**:
  - Banks typically offer an additional interest rate premium of **0.50% per annum** on Fixed Deposits and Recurring Deposits to senior citizens (individuals aged 60 years and above).
- **Premature Withdrawal of FDs**:
  - Banks may levy a penalty (generally 0.5% to 1.0% below the applicable contracted rate) for premature liquidation of term deposits, except where explicitly exempted by the bank's deposit policy.

## 6. Grievance Redressal & Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS)
- **First Escalation**:
  - The customer must first register their grievance with the bank's internal Principal Nodal Officer or Customer Grievance Cell.
- **Escalation to Ombudsman**:
  - If the bank fails to resolve the complaint within **30 days**, or if the customer is dissatisfied with the resolution provided, the customer can file a complaint with the RBI Integrated Ombudsman free of charge.
  - Complaints can be submitted online at **https://cms.rbi.org.in** or via toll-free telephone helpline **14448**.
